# Marketing Plan

## Target users

* Farmers and land managers using smartphones in the field.
* Tomato and potato growers.
* Crops growers in the future.


## Problem and value

* **Problem:** Plant diseases are difficult to spot early and costly to treat when advanced.

* **Value:** Rootine provides AI leaf diagnosis and early weather risk alerts, preventing crop loss before diseases spread.

## Channels

* **Facebook Groups:** Regional agricultural communities where farmers ask for treatment advice.

* **Local Partnerships:** Flyers with QR codes at checkout counters of agricultural supply and seed stores.

* **Agricultural Fairs:** Live demonstrations where farmers scan a leaf with their own phones.
  

## Key messages

* Outsmart the Farmer's Almanac with AI-powered precision.
* Turn your phone into your own agronomist.
* Scan in time, save your harvest.
* Stop guessing. Start scanning.
* Don't let the weather surprise you. Get early disease warnings for your fields.
