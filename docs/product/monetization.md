# Monetization

## Who pays

The primary paying customers are farmers who use Rootine to monitor crop health and protect their parcels.

Potential customers include:
- Individual farmers who want to monitor their crops and detect diseases early.
- Small and medium-sized farms that need regular crop monitoring.
- Larger farms managing multiple parcels and needing centralized monitoring.

## Pricing ideas

Rootine will use a subscription-based pricing model with two payment options:

### Monthly subscription
- Customers pay a fixed subscription fee every month.
- The subscription can be cancelled according to the cancellation policy.

### Annual subscription
- Customers can choose to pay for an entire year.
- The annual subscription is **25% cheaper than paying for 12 months individually**.
- This provides an incentive for customers to commit to Rootine for a longer period.

### Free trial
- The first month is free.
- Customers can try Rootine's features before committing to a paid subscription.
- After the free month, the selected subscription plan becomes active.

## Costs
Rootine's main costs come from hosting the application, storing uploaded images, running AI predictions and sending notifications.

The operational costs will mainly depend on:

- The number of registered farmers.
- The number and size of parcels managed by each farmer.
- The number of leaf photos uploaded and AI predictions performed.
- The frequency of daily risk calculations.
- The amount of image storage required.
- The number of email notifications sent.
